Support

We are a small team and we read every message.

Write to us

Email hello@onelifestack.com. Tell us your phone model and Android version, and what you were doing; a screenshot of the error helps. Never send us your footage: we cannot receive it, and we do not need it.

Common questions

Is My Films free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing to buy.

Does it upload my videos anywhere?

It cannot. My Films does not ask for the internet permission, so Android will not let it open a connection. Editing and rendering happen on the phone's own chips.

Where do my films go?

Exports are saved to Movies › My Films in your gallery. They are ordinary MP4 files you own, and they stay there even if you delete the app.

Will it change my original clips?

Never. Every export is a new rendering. Your originals are only read.

What can it export?

H.264 or HEVC video with AAC audio, at 720p, 1080p, 2K or 4K, at the source frame rate or 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60 fps. Older or smaller phones may not manage 4K; try 1080p if an export fails.

Why does it ask for photos and music access?

To show your gallery and your songs inside the app. Both are optional: the system picker and a file picker work without them, and on Android 14 and newer you can share only the items you choose.

Why does it show a notification?

An export keeps running when you leave the app, and Android requires a notification for that. It shows the progress and tells you when the film is ready.

Is there a watermark or a time limit?

No watermark, no limit on length. A long 4K export takes a while and the phone may run warm; that is the rendering, not a limit.

How do I install the beta?

Open the Get My Films section on your phone, tap Download, and open the file when it finishes. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or Files app once; say yes, then tap Install. It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Is the beta safe?

It is the same app we will put on Google Play, built by us and signed with a beta key, with the same promises: no account, no ads, no tracking, no internet permission. Check the file with the SHA-256 checksum shown next to the download, and only install it from this site.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 (Oreo) and newer. Hardware video decoding and encoding are used, so what your phone's camera can record, My Films can usually edit.

How do I get help or share an idea?

Open Settings › Help in the app, or write to hello@onelifestack.com.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Erase everything removes every project and the thumbnail cache. Uninstalling removes the rest. Your exported films stay in your gallery; delete them there if you want them gone.

An export failed?

The app says why in plain words: a clip the phone cannot decode, a size it cannot encode, a file that was deleted, or no space left. Try 1080p and H.264 first; they work on every phone we know of. If it still fails, write to us with the message shown.
