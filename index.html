How it works

Three steps to a film.

  1. Pick your clips

    Choose videos and photos from your gallery, in the order you want them. Start from a template for a square post, a story or a widescreen film.

  2. Shape the film

    Trim and reorder on a timeline with real frames. Change the speed, add a filter, a title, music, and a fade between clips. The preview plays exactly what the export will render.

  3. Export and share

    Pick 720p to 4K, the frame rate and the quality. The film lands in Movies › My Films in your gallery, ready to share from any app you like.

Tools

Everything a short film needs.

Every tool previews live and applies as one undo step, so trying something costs nothing.

Trim, split, reorder

Drag the handles, split at the playhead, hold a clip to move it. Photos sit in the sequence like any clip.

Speed and curves

From 0.1× to 100×, with the pitch kept if you like. Or draw a curve: Montage, Hero, Bullet, Jump cut, Flash in, or your own.

Filters and adjust

Nine looks with an intensity slider, plus brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth and hue.

Keyframes

Animate scale, position, rotation and opacity over time, with easing. Ken Burns on a photo takes two keyframes.

Titles

Four fonts, colour, outline, shadow and background. Fade, rise, pop or typewriter animations. Drag the title where you want it.

Music

A song from your phone or a file, with volume, fade in and fade out, and a start point.

Frame

Crop to a shape, rotate, and fit or fill the film's aspect: 1:1, 9:16, 16:9 and more.

Export

H.264 or HEVC at 720p, 1080p, 2K or 4K. Keeps rendering in the background with a progress notification.

Speed

Ramps, slow motion, jump cuts.

A constant speed is one slider. A speed curve is a line you draw: slow into the moment, race through the rest. Presets get you started, and the timeline shows the clip at its new length so the cut stays honest.

Keep the pitch if there is a voice, or let it drop for the effect.

Keyframes and titles

Make it move.

Put a keyframe where the clip should start and another where it should end; My Films eases scale, position, rotation and opacity between them. A slow push into a photo takes seconds to set up.

Titles come in four fonts with colour, outline, shadow and background, and animate in with a fade, a rise, a pop or a typewriter. Drag them straight onto the preview.

Music and export

Your song, your film, your gallery.

Add a song from your phone, set where it starts, how loud it is and how it fades. Then export: 720p for a quick share, 1080p for most things, 4K when it matters. H.264 plays everywhere; HEVC makes smaller files.

The export runs in the background with a progress notification, and the film lands in Movies › My Films.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is My Films free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing to buy.

Does it upload my videos anywhere?

It cannot. My Films does not ask for the internet permission, so Android will not let it open a connection. Editing and rendering happen on the phone's own chips.

Where do my films go?

Exports are saved to Movies › My Films in your gallery. They are ordinary MP4 files you own, and they stay there even if you delete the app.

Will it change my original clips?

Never. Every export is a new rendering. Your originals are only read.

What can it export?

H.264 or HEVC video with AAC audio, at 720p, 1080p, 2K or 4K, at the source frame rate or 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60 fps. Older or smaller phones may not manage 4K; try 1080p if an export fails.

Why does it ask for photos and music access?

To show your gallery and your songs inside the app. Both are optional: the system picker and a file picker work without them, and on Android 14 and newer you can share only the items you choose.

Why does it show a notification?

An export keeps running when you leave the app, and Android requires a notification for that. It shows the progress and tells you when the film is ready.

Is there a watermark or a time limit?

No watermark, no limit on length. A long 4K export takes a while and the phone may run warm; that is the rendering, not a limit.

How do I install the beta?

Open the Get My Films section on your phone, tap Download, and open the file when it finishes. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or Files app once; say yes, then tap Install. It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Is the beta safe?

It is the same app we will put on Google Play, built by us and signed with a beta key, with the same promises: no account, no ads, no tracking, no internet permission. Check the file with the SHA-256 checksum shown next to the download, and only install it from this site.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 (Oreo) and newer. Hardware video decoding and encoding are used, so what your phone's camera can record, My Films can usually edit.

How do I get help or share an idea?

Open Settings › Help in the app, or write to hello@onelifestack.com.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Erase everything removes every project and the thumbnail cache. Uninstalling removes the rest. Your exported films stay in your gallery; delete them there if you want them gone.
