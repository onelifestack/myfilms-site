Press kit

My Films is a free, private video editor for Android. For anything else, write to hello@onelifestack.com.

In one line

Edit videos on your phone and nothing leaves it: trim, speed curves, filters, keyframes, titles and music, exported up to 4K, with no account, no ads and no internet permission.

Facts

  • Name: My Films
  • Maker: OneLifeStack
  • Platform: Android 8.0 and newer
  • Price: free, no ads, no purchases, no watermark
  • Privacy: no account, no analytics, no internet permission
  • Engine: AndroidX Media3 (hardware decoding and encoding)
  • Beta: coming soon at myfilms.onelifestack.com/#get
